# Phase 1 Summary - Asset Criticality & Risk

The highest risks are driven by human-facing systems rather than infrastructure complexity. 
Email, messaging platforms, and browser-based financial systems represent the most critical attack paths due to high usage and limited security controls.

Financial systems and the network gateway represent high-impact assets, while unmanaged endpoints and communication tools increase likelihood of compromise.
These findings justify prioritizing visibility and monitoring over perimeter hardening in early security efforts.

| Tier         | Description                                  |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Critical** | Direct financial loss or business disruption |
| **High**     | Operational impact, data exposure            |
| **Medium**   | Productivity impact                          |
| **Low**      | Limited or indirect impact                   |

Risk Scoring Model:

We use a qualitative risk model:

Risk = Likelihood × Impact
