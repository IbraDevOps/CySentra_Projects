# Threat Model — Abra Traders

## External Threats
- Phishing via email
- Phishing and impersonation via WhatsApp
- Credential harvesting
- Malware delivery
- Banking fraud
- Account takeover

## Internal / Environmental Risks
- Compromised endpoint enabling lateral movement
- Weakly secured printers or CCTV devices
- Credential reuse across systems
- Absence of centralized logging and monitoring
- Human error due to lack of security awareness

## High-Risk Assets
- Internet banking portals
- Sage Accounting system
- Google Sheets containing financial data
- Email accounts
