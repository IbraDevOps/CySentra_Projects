# Asset Criticality — Phase 1

| Asset                    | Description                         | Tier      | Rationale                               |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------------|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| Internet Banking Portals | Regional banking platforms          | Critical  | Direct access to funds                  |
| Accounting System        | Financial records and payments      | Critical  | Integrity and fraud risk                |
| Cloud Spreadsheets       | Financial and operational data      | High      | Data leakage and manipulation           |
| Email Accounts           | Primary communication channel       | High      | Phishing and account takeover           |
| WhatsApp                 | Internal coordination               | High      | Social engineering and impersonation    |
| Employee Endpoints       | Laptops and PCs                     | High      | Malware entry point                     |
| Smartphones              | Personal devices used for work      | Medium    | Limited control and lateral movement    |
| Network Router           | Gateway to network                  | Critical  | Single point of failure                 |
| Printers                 | Network-connected devices           | Low       | Peripheral attack surface               |
| CCTV Cameras             | IoT surveillance devices            | Low       | Weak credentials and pivot potential    |
